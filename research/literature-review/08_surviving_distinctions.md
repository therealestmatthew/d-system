# Surviving distinctions

This file follows `07_anti_novelty_case.md`'s component-by-component decomposition and carries
forward only what that decomposition left standing, sorted into the methodology's three categories
(`CLAUDE.md` §13-14, as applied to the eleven hypotheses): already known, known component with a new
integration, and possibly requiring new mechanism design. Status labels are taken verbatim from
`06_hypothesis_tests.md` and are not re-derived, narrowed, or blended here — this file's job is to
place each hypothesis in the category its existing status implies, not to re-judge the status
itself. `NOVEL` remains unavailable to every hypothesis in this review; nothing below is presented
as novel, and nothing below is a claim that D-System should be renamed or reframed — per
`CLAUDE.md` §2, that revision happens only in later synthesis, by other dispatches.

The same coverage caveat that opens `07_anti_novelty_case.md` applies here without restatement in
full: 336 of 400 collision candidates surfaced by this campaign's searches were never deep-read,
including 2 of the top-prescore band's 32 candidates — `dhar-vaidhyanathan-varma-agenticakm-2026`
and `epistemic-sybil-resistance-multiplying-agents-2026`, each a documented near-duplicate of a
source this campaign did deep-read, under the partner ids `dhar-vaidhyanathan-varma-agenticakm-2026-arxiv`
and `epistemic-sybil-resistance-bara-2026` respectively — and no claim of search saturation is made for
any hypothesis. Every category placement below is bounded by what this campaign actually found, not
by what the full candidate pool would show if read.

## Category 1 — Already known

Hypotheses whose core mechanism `06_hypothesis_tests.md` assesses as `LIKELY_ALREADY_KNOWN`: the
field's coverage is dense enough, and close enough to D-System's literal claim, that treating the
mechanism as an open question would misstate the evidence.

### H2 — Typed transition semantics as reasoning memory

**Status:** `LIKELY_ALREADY_KNOWN`. Thirteen matrix rows challenge H2, two at the matrix's ceiling
component-overlap score of 5 (`graph-native-cognitive-memory-belief-revision-semantics-2026`,
`jansen-bosch-architecture-as-decisions-wicsa-2005`) — both disputed on independent second review
to 4/3 and 3/2 respectively, disputes recorded but not applied per the owner's ruling
(`06_hypothesis_tests.md`'s H2 block). The claim traces through AGM belief-revision
logic (1985), Snodgrass's transaction-time tables (1999), the ADR tradition (Jansen & Bosch 2005,
formally extended by Zimmermann et al. 2009), W3C PROV-O (2013), and multiple 2025-2026 production
agent-memory systems (Zep/Graphiti, Kumiho) — see `07_anti_novelty_case.md` §§2-4. Per
`06_hypothesis_tests.md`'s own qualification, no single source combines transition-preservation with
D-System's full H1 state model simultaneously; on H2's literal, narrower claim, the field's coverage
is thorough and multi-lineage.

### H4 — Independence-aware convergence

**Status:** `LIKELY_ALREADY_KNOWN`. `06_hypothesis_tests.md`'s H4 block, re-derived clean-room by
`LIT-09 H4R` (2026-09-19) from the evidence alone, assesses the review-instructions' general phrasing
and the frozen register's graph-topological phrasing at the same status, retiring the prior
reconciliation's split between them. The graph-topological family the prior reconciliation had found
too thin — `epistemic-sybil-resistance-bara-2026`'s provenance-DAG discount kappa_m alone, plus
`barakat-corroboration-provenance-patterns-tapp2017`'s unimplemented Eq.8 sketch — has grown, via this
campaign's own collision searches (`LIT-09-S034/S035`, `LIT-09-S052/S053`), to three independent 2026
instantiations: `grading-narrators-isnad-rijal-claim-provenance-2026` (ISNAD) and
`not-all-agreement-counts-as-corroboration-2026` (PACT) join bara-2026. ISNAD is the strongest
challenger because it combines all three elements the frozen register's falsification clause names in
one source: a derivation-chain topology (the isnad), an explicit computed discount for shared lineage
(Sec.4.3's muta ba'at cross-chain check), and a confirmed mixed human-agent narrator scope
(`narrator_type` spans source|scraper|model|human, second-review-confirmed). This sits atop a
forty-year cross-domain ancestor base: Bayesian testimony epistemology (Goldman 2001, extended by
Mayo-Wilson 2014), distributed multi-version fault tolerance (Eckhardt & Lee 1985 -> Townend et al.
2005, cited into barakat-2017), and sensor fusion/belief-function combination (Julier & Uhlmann 1997,
Denoeux 2008, Josang 2010/2016, feeding both PACT and ISNAD's own discount arithmetic). Per
`06_hypothesis_tests.md`, this is an established, independently-recurring pattern rather than one
narrow preprint, past the same maturity threshold this file uses elsewhere (H3, H9) to call a field
thoroughly known. No single row reaches a confirmed component_overlap of 5, so this is not read as
total, unqualified prior anticipation of D-System's own knowledge-state-graph instantiation — but the
specific combination the frozen register names is present, together, in one deep-read primary source.

## Category 2 — Known component, new integration

Hypotheses whose components `06_hypothesis_tests.md` assesses as `KNOWN_COMPONENT_NEW_INTEGRATION`:
every piece the hypothesis needs exists and is mature, but no single found source demonstrates them
combined the way D-System proposes. Per the campaign's own method, this is the expected shape of a
successful recombination finding, not a research gap.

### H3 — Provenance as conflict-resolution input

**Status:** `KNOWN_COMPONENT_NEW_INTEGRATION`. Rich, typed, multi-actor provenance representation is
thoroughly known (PROV-O, EVI, log-is-the-agent, extending-nanopublications, DIVE — see
`06_hypothesis_tests.md`'s H3 block and `07_anti_novelty_case.md` §2). Automated conflict
resolution is also known: AGM's axiomatically-constrained entrenchment rule (1985 — §4) and, at
real deployed scale, extending-nanopublications' sufficiency/consistency threshold test — but both
resolve disagreement from evidence quantity alone, never drawing on the actor-identity/authority
dimensions that make provenance "rich." No source combines rich, authority-bearing provenance with
an arbitration rule that actually uses that authority: EVI's authors state directly that challenge
resolution "ultimately require[s] human judgment"; TGMS explicitly disables provenance-based
write-back "pending policies"; DIVE's refutation mechanism is manual and user-triggered. Per
`06_hypothesis_tests.md`, this is worth recording as more than incidental — of the three
2020-2026 teams that declined to automate, and the one (nanopublications) that did automate but
without engaging its own actor-trust machinery, the pattern is some evidence that
authority-weighted automated arbitration carries a correctness or liability risk builders did not
want to accept, not proof the combination is hard to build.

### H5 — Topology-aware context transfer

**Status:** `KNOWN_COMPONENT_NEW_INTEGRATION`. Retrieval beyond plain semantic similarity —
incorporating graph topology, temporal recency, reasoning lineage, and evidence provenance — is
thoroughly established (Zep/Graphiti's traversal-plus-recency pipeline, EM-LLM's peer-reviewed
episodic retrieval, solozobov's explicit memory-tier context compiler — `07_anti_novelty_case.md`
§§1, 7). But H5 as worded lists seven specific dimensions (state, lineage, evidence, dissent,
authority, convergence, unresolved uncertainty), and per `06_hypothesis_tests.md` no single source
combines more than three or four of them; dissent, authority, and convergence specifically as
first-class retrieval inputs were not found anywhere at this detail. The mechanism family is known;
the specific seven-dimension combination is an untested combination, not a demonstrated one.

### H6 — Integrated human-agent collective knowledge evolution

**Status:** `KNOWN_COMPONENT_NEW_INTEGRATION`. OmniScientist ships a full, empirically-evaluated
instance of integrated human-agent collective knowledge evolution in the scientific-research domain
(0.22 accuracy for Human-AI Collaboration Mode vs. 0.10 Human Solo vs. 0.00 AI Solo on the same
case study). Per its own architecture, it is itself a recombination — its ledger mechanism a
scientific-credit specialization of nanopublication/PROV-O patterns (`07_anti_novelty_case.md` §12),
its communication layer built atop MCP/A2A rather than invented. No single source found substantially
subsumes D-System's specific two-system, dual-domain pairing (knowledge construction paired with a
software development lifecycle under one collective-evolution framing), but each half of that
pairing has an independently demonstrated, evaluated analog in a different domain — OmniScientist for
the knowledge-construction side, AgenticAKM and the ADR lineage for the development side. That is a
recombination of two already-demonstrated domain-specific integrations, exactly what H6 anticipates
when it concedes "even if primitives are known."

### H8 — Phase-bounded context construction (frozen-register phrasing)

**Status:** `KNOWN_COMPONENT_NEW_INTEGRATION` for the frozen register's fuller phrasing — context
assembled from persistent lineage *and* execution results consolidated back into persistent
*knowledge*, not merely opaque state — kept separate from the review-instructions phrasing already
placed in Category 1. The assembly half is well covered: solozobov's context compiler, Aporia's
Decision Bank, LangGraph's parent-chain loading (`07_anti_novelty_case.md` §§3, 7). No source
demonstrates the consolidation half operating over persistent, genuinely epistemic,
knowledge-bearing memory rather than opaque execution state: LangGraph's `channel_values` are "opaque
key-value pairs" over generic application state, an absence the field's own literature names
directly (`burns-groth-agentic-ontological-notebook-memory-2026`, characterizing LangGraph as
treating "persisted state as opaque key-value pairs rather than typed ontological structures").
Consolidation into knowledge-bearing memory, combined with assembly from the same, is the frozen
register's distinguishing addition and is not demonstrated combined in any one system found.

### H10 — Epistemic blast-radius analysis

**Status:** `KNOWN_COMPONENT_NEW_INTEGRATION`. MemTX is a real, machine-verified system performing
the core mechanism — epistemic retraction cascading, correctness-verified, to every downstream
derived record and tool side effect, proven over 5.5 million enumerated protocol states
(`07_anti_novelty_case.md` §§4, 13). This is not a proposal; it is a proven property. Its scope,
though, is one memory store's own derived records and tool effects, not D-System's proposed
cross-lifecycle span into decisions, requirements, plans, artifacts, and tests — and the field's own
systematic literature survey (134 studies) independently confirms this broader, cross-lifecycle
capability is the least mature, least tooled activity in the software-assumptions field despite
well-documented need (`07_anti_novelty_case.md` §9). Read together: the underlying mechanism is
proven and known; the specific cross-lifecycle scope D-System proposes is a recombination the field's
own survey confirms nobody has built yet, not a mechanism nobody knows how to build.

## Category 3 — Possibly requiring new mechanism design

Hypotheses `06_hypothesis_tests.md` assesses as `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` — the status the file's own
two-condition rule reserves for cases where the search ran (condition 1: a dedicated, multi-strategy,
primary-text-verified search) but did not turn up a mature, on-topic comparator family whose closest
member instantiates the same mechanism (condition 2). This is not `POTENTIALLY_DISTINCT`, and it is
categorically not `NOVEL` — the status records that this campaign's search could not resolve the
question either way, not that the mechanism has been shown absent from the wider field. Placement in
this category is the most that this campaign's evidence supports; it is a statement about what a
future, more targeted search would need to either close or confirm, not a distinctiveness claim.

### H1 — Multi-dimensional state model

**Status:** `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`. Six candidates were found and deep-read for the orthogonal
ontological+epistemic+lifecycle triple; per `07_anti_novelty_case.md` §7, two of them
(`mythologiq-agent-memory-oss`, `subit-wiki-epistemic-hmm-oss`) come structurally closer than any
other source in the whole campaign, without either combining the right axis *content* with genuine
independence and an established, mature comparator family. MythologIQ has two of the right axes
(type, lifecycle stage) as independent enums but distributes the third (epistemic classification)
across four separate fields rather than one independently-varying value; subit-wiki has three
genuinely independent axes but of the wrong content (discourse stance, not
ontological/epistemic/lifecycle); toki proves three axes orthogonal with real rigor but the wrong
triple entirely (isolation/schema/provenance, a bitemporal concurrency-control formalism). The
closest candidates are ten-weeks-old, single-author, 12-star, unreviewed repositories — this
campaign's search of that specific, thin, very-recent corner of GitHub and arXiv does not establish
that no established framework exists elsewhere; it establishes that this corner does not have one.

### H11 — Runtime-to-knowledge closure

**Status:** `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`. The awareness-requirements lineage (2010-2023, peer-reviewed at
ICSE and SEAMS) is a genuinely mature comparator family — this campaign's search satisfies condition
1 in full, including a multi-paper backward citation chain — but its best-implemented member
(EvoReqs, 2012) closes a structurally different loop: rule-triggered mutation over a closed,
analyst-authored vocabulary, not evidence weighed into a provenance-bearing knowledge revision.
Every member of the family that *is* aimed at the same mechanism D-System proposes
(sawyer-bencomo 2010, souza-lapouchnian 2011, and the brand-new krentsel-agarwal-cemri 2026) states
it explicitly as unbuilt future work or a research agenda, never evaluated. Per
`06_hypothesis_tests.md`, a mature field that has spent over a decade naming this exact gap, and
still has not built a working instance of it, is evidence worth weighing in synthesis — but under
this file's own rule it is not sufficient on its own to call the mechanism absent from the wider
field. This is the closer call of the two `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` verdicts: H1 fails on comparator
maturity, H11 fails on mechanism match within a mature comparator family, a different failure shape
worth keeping distinct rather than flattening into an identical reason.
