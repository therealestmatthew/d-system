# Adversarial findings

The largest architectural risk is treating a useful local workflow implementation as evidence that the proposed knowledge-state architecture already works. The implementation offers strong baselines, but no measured advantage for three-axis classification, provenance-weighted convergence, graph context or epistemic impact analysis.
