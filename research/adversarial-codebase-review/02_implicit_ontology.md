# Implicit ontology

Classification concerns implementation semantics, not whether the proposed concept is philosophically correct. `ACCIDENTAL_DESIGN` denotes a structural consequence without a supporting design decision found, not a claim to know the author's intent. Confidence/alternatives for every cited observation are in the [evidence register](../evidence/code-evidence.md); confidence in *intent* is lower where explicitly stated.
