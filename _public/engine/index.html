How to read this page

G1 shows ideas captured, not a queue: the owner approves an idea in conversation, and the sanctioned writer records it only after that.

Stages 5 and 6 show not recorded: adversarial reviews and phase-fit checks are written into session records as prose, with no structured record to count.

Stage 4 and the G3 queue are the same plans by construction. Plan front matter does not record whether a draft is still being written, under review, or waiting for approval, so every draft plan is counted at stage 4 and listed at G3.

Phase links come from backlog phases' ideas field, which names the ideas each phase's own scope, acceptance and next_action mention.

Blocked phases, not counted in any stage: phase-irs-11.
