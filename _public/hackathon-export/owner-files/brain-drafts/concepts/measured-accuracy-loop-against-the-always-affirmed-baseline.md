The accuracy loop measures the product on published appeal-board decisions. Code removes the outcome from each decision before the product sees it.

Results are reported as recall, lift and flag rate on reversals, beside the "always affirmed" baseline. Figure 4 states these are reported results, not claimed benefit. Calibration uses DOL appeal reversal rates and BAM improper-denial rates.

The figure's dashed path runs from the accuracy loop through prompt and retrieval tuning back to the reasoning core.
