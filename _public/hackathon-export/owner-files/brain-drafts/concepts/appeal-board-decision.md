General knowledge, not stated in ARCH-002 or Figure 4: an appeal-board decision is the ruling of a state's appeal authority on a determination that a claimant or employer has appealed. It either affirms or reverses the earlier decision.

In this architecture, published appeal-board decisions are the labels in the validation data. Code removes the outcome before the decisions are used as input to the measured accuracy loop.
