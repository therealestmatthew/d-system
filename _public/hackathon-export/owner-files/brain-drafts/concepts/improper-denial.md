General knowledge, not stated in ARCH-002 or Figure 4: an improper denial is a decision that refuses benefits to a claimant who was eligible for them.

The Bidirectional Flagging agent assesses improper-denial risk as the second of its two independent lenses, separate from improper-payment risk. BAM improper-denial rates are part of the calibration data.
