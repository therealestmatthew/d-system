The product checks for errors in both directions: paying a claim that should not be paid (improper payment) and denying a claim that should be paid (improper denial).

The Bidirectional Flagging agent assesses the two risks as two independent lenses. The examiner workbench shows each case's flag status in the prioritized queue.
