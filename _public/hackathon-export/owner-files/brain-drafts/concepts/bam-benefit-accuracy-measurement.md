Figure 4 and ARCH-002 use only the abbreviation BAM. General knowledge, not stated in ARCH-002 or Figure 4: BAM stands for Benefit Accuracy Measurement, the DOL quality-control program that samples unemployment insurance payments and denials to estimate how often they are wrong.

In this architecture, BAM improper-denial rates are part of the calibration data for the accuracy loop.
