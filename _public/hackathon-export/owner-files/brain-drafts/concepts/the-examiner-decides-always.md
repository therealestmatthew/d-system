Figure 4 states: "The examiner decides. Always." Claude recommends; it never determines.

The examiner workbench shows the case summary beside the findings, with every item citation-traceable, and the examiner accepts, edits or rejects them with the drafted notice alongside. Escalation to a supervisor is recommended for high-risk, ambiguous or suspected-fraud cases. Disagreeing with the system never triggers review on its own.
