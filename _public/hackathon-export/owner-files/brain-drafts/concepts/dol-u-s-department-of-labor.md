DOL is the U.S. Department of Labor, the federal agency that oversees the state unemployment insurance programs (general knowledge).

In this architecture, Federal DOL guidance is part of the legal authority corpus, and DOL appeal reversal rates are part of the calibration data for the accuracy loop.
