D‑SystemProposed knowledge architecture
    Working research architecture

    An append‑only architecture for collective human‑agent reasoning

    D‑System aims to represent not only what is known, but what kind of knowledge a state represents, how its truth status should be regarded, where it sits in an idea‑to‑action lifecycle, how it changed from prior states, who or what caused those changes, what evidence supported them, and how that lineage should affect later retrieval.

    Status. Everything on this page is the architecture as proposed. The source documents describe themselves as “proposed research, not authoritative descriptions of the implementation” and their vocabulary as “working definitions, not claims of novelty.” Read it as a design under test, not a description of shipped behaviour.

    01

    The core distinction#

    Three things that ordinary systems collapse into one, and the reason D‑System keeps them apart.

    ▾
    State describes what the system represents at a point in reasoning history. Transition describes how one state became another. Provenance describes the lineage and responsibility of that transition.

    NodeState

    A semantically meaningful representation of something at a particular point in its reasoning or operational history. Classified across three independent dimensions and never overwritten.

    Edge — first classTransition

    A typed transformation by which one or more states produce, influence, supersede, or lead to subsequent states. A transition is a research object even where a graph API exposes it as an edge.

    Attached to transitionsProvenance

    Origin and derivational lineage — actors, evidence, method, authority, time, upstream dependencies. The record of who caused a change and on what basis.

    Why the separation matters

    A single frozen state is not sufficient to reconstruct change. Historical meaning often resides in the delta between states and in the transition that produced the delta. A system that mutates records in place keeps the answer and discards the reasoning; a system that keeps states but not typed transitions keeps the reasoning as unstructured text.

    02

    Three‑axis classification#

    Every knowledge state is classified independently on what it is, what its truth status is, and where it sits in its lifecycle. Pick one value from each column to compose a state.

    ▾
    Open research issue. Temporal validity should likely be represented separately from epistemic status. A proposition can have been true within a prior interval without becoming a falsified proposition — and the lifecycle is not assumed to be strictly linear: retrospective insights may trigger reconsideration and produce new generative seeds.
    03

    The state‑transition model#

    The lifecycle is treated as a graph of typed transitions between retained states, not a set of mutable records. Hover or click any node.

    ▾
    Illustrative path — the lifecycle is a graph, not a queue. RECONSIDER closes the loop.

    A decision need not be a node

    DECIDE can be the transition that produces a Strategic Directive, rather than a classification the directive carries. This is the single most consequential modelling choice on this page: it moves rationale, alternatives, authority and dissent out of a record’s fields and onto the edge that created it.

    Nothing is overwritten

    Instead of mutating Hypothesis → Ground Truth and losing the former state, D‑System records a new state and the transition between them. Supersession is asserted, not performed by deletion.

    Questions provenance must answer
    • Who initiated the transition?
    • Who participated, and who approved?
    • Was it individual, collective, delegated, or automated?
    • Which evidence was used?
    • Which methods produced that evidence?
    • What prior reasoning influenced the actor?
    • Under what authority was the transition made?
    • Were multiple supposedly independent transitions actually derived from the same source?
    Authority is not reliability. A manager may have authority to approve a deployment without being the most technically reliable source on database performance. Actor identity, actor type, domain authority, authorization and historical reliability should not be collapsed into a single scalar weight.
    Formal shape

    StateS = (O, E, L, Content, TemporalScope, Metadata)

    Three classifications plus content, the interval over which it applies, and metadata.

    TransitionSt —[T, P]→ St+1

    T is the typed transition; P is its provenance.

    ProvenanceP = (Actors, Evidence, Method, Authority, Time, Lineage)

    Working abstractions, not yet a formal ontology or mathematical theory.

    Conflict, authority and convergence#

    Losing branches are preserved rather than deleted, and agreement is discounted by how much of it is derivative.

    ▾

           Convergence,
           TemporalRelevance,

    Five agents repeating one source should not be treated as five independent confirmations.

    Repeated arrival at the same conclusion strengthens confidence only to the extent that the paths are meaningfully independent. Independent human reasoning, separate experiments, telemetry, and distinct agents using non‑overlapping evidence may constitute stronger convergence. Independence is a property of the derivation topology, not of the count.

    08

    Memory, context and retrieval#

    The working hypothesis that memory lives in lineage rather than in a node type — and the retrieval primitive that would follow from it.

    ▾
    Nodes preserve states; transition lineage preserves much of what should be understood as memory.

    What the hypothesis claims

    Richer memory may emerge from retained states + transitions + temporal ordering + provenance + relationships + retrieval capability — rather than from a dedicated memory record type.

    What it explicitly does not claim

    That all memory must literally be implemented as graph edges. It is a conceptual hypothesis to test against memory systems, temporal knowledge representation, event sourcing, provenance research and cognitive models.

    Stored knowledge is not active context
    The intended retrieval primitive

    Richer than nearest‑neighbour semantic search. An agent should be able to retrieve:

    Current dominant stateReasoning lineageSupporting evidenceDissenting branchesActor provenanceUnresolved hypothesesSuperseded directivesConfidence & authority signalsRelevant temporal context

    Example desired outputWhat a topology-aware answer looks like

    “PostgreSQL is the current strategic direction, reached through multiple evaluations, affirmed by authorized architecture actors, superseding an earlier alternative, with one unresolved concern around long‑term traversal performance.”

    The research question is whether graph topology, provenance, and epistemic/lifecycle state can improve downstream reasoning compared with standard document or vector‑memory retrieval.

    The two‑system architecture#

    Cognition and software execution solve different problems. D‑System proposes not collapsing them into one undifferentiated graph — and modelling the boundary between them explicitly.

    ▾

    The boundary — converting reasoned commitment into implementable intent
    A decision does not automatically equal a requirement.

    What a decision may actually do

    This translation should be modelled explicitly rather than hidden inside documents. A single decision may:

    create requirementsmodify requirementsreject requirementsintroduce constraintsprioritize requirementsselect architectureauthorize implementationsupersede a prior directive
    The full lifecycle — twelve stages, one loop
    The reality feedback loop
    Separation of truth, intent and realization

    Three layers D‑System proposes never to collapse. The worked example shows why: each line is a different kind of claim, and merging any two makes the last line unstateable.

    LayerAsksWorked example
    EpistemicWhat do we believe or know?“We believe users need offline support.”
    IntentWhat have we decided should be true?“The product shall support offline operation.”
    RealizationWhat was actually implemented?“Offline caching exists in release 2.4.”
    ObservedWhat actually happened?“Offline synchronization fails on conflict.”
    ReflectionWhat does that mean?“The implementation does not fully satisfy the intended requirement.”
    Reality → Representation → Reasoning → Commitment → Specification → Execution → Realization → Observation → Experience → Revised Representation

    D‑System’s research question is whether preserving explicit provenance‑bearing transitions across this entire loop improves human‑agent development, context reconstruction, change‑impact analysis and system explainability.

    Epistemic blast radius

    The set of downstream decisions, requirements, specifications, plans, artifacts, functionality or conclusions whose justification depends materially on a state that has been revised, falsified, weakened or superseded. Click the assumption to propagate.

    13

    Falsifiable hypotheses#

    Twelve claims written to be disproved. Each carries the condition that would falsify it — the point of the exercise is to make novelty testable rather than asserted.

    ▾
    No novelty claim is established. The working hypothesis is that individual components overlap with substantial prior work, while possible differentiation may lie in the integration of typed multi‑dimensional knowledge states + explicit state‑transition semantics + provenance‑weighted human/agent convergence and conflict + topology‑aware context transfer. This must be aggressively tested against prior art.
    Required outcome categories

    Each hypothesis must ultimately be classified as exactly one of the following. NOVEL is not an available verdict without a substantially stronger search and expert review.

    LIKELY_ALREADY_KNOWNKNOWN_COMPONENT_NEW_INTEGRATIONPOTENTIALLY_DISTINCTINSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE
