D‑SystemResearch pack protocol
    Governed research methodology · GOV‑009

    One session manufactures the pack. The campaign spends it — against its own thesis.

    A research pack is the complete governed artifact set a literature‑review or research campaign runs from — scope record, hypothesis register, search‑domain matrix, evidence contract, delegation prompts, coordinator prompt and kick‑off record. This page is the protocol for manufacturing and spending one: the prompt‑pack pipeline, with research discipline in place of build discipline.

    Authority. The authoritative text is the research pack protocol (GOV‑009, docs/08-governance/), which applies the prompt‑pack planning protocol (GOV‑008) to research work. On content method — what to search, how to score overlap, what an evidence row contains — the methodology files under research/ win. This page is a rendering of the protocol, not a second copy of the rules.
    01

    The core split#

    Planning a campaign and running its searches are different kinds of work, done in different sessions, with a hard boundary between them.

    ▾
    The planning session manufactures the pack. The execution session spends it — via verbatim dispatch. If the planning session did not write the prompt, the execution session does not send it.

    Session onePlanning

    Interactive, owner‑in‑the‑loop. Ratifies the scope and the hypothesis set through question batches, investigates the research directory, and writes every prompt the campaign will need — through the eight‑stage pipeline below.

    The boundaryVerbatim dispatch

    Every search, extraction and review prompt is pre‑crafted and idempotent. A missing prompt is a blocking finding for the owner, never something to improvise. This is what makes a campaign resumable, auditable and reproducible.

    Session twoExecution

    A coordinator claims nothing, searches nothing, writes no findings, and dispatches the pack’s sections in order. Every re‑run of the same coordinator prompt is a resume, not a restart.

    Why research needs the boundary more, not less

    A build that improvises mid‑run produces bad code; a review that improvises mid‑run produces bad epistemics. An agent that writes its own search prompts while collecting evidence can quietly steer the queries toward the answer it is forming — narrowing a hypothesis to dodge a collision, skipping the synonym that would find the falsifier. Freezing the prompts before the first search removes that degree of freedom: the campaign attacks the thesis with queries designed before anyone knew what they would return.

    The word rule. The artifact set is always a research pack — never a “prompt pack”, which names the multi‑agent build methodology (GOV‑008) this protocol is derived from.
    02

    Process and content are separate layers#

    GOV‑009 governs sessions, gates and sign‑offs. What a search covers and what an evidence row contains was already written — and stays authoritative.

    ▾
    Where the protocol and the research/ methodology files conflict on content method, the methodology files win. Where they are silent on process, the protocol governs.
    No improvised methodology. A campaign that needs a different content methodology writes its own before the pack is drafted — it does not invent one mid‑search. The pack is manufactured against a methodology that already exists.
    03

    The eight‑stage pipeline#

    Stages produce artifacts; gates freeze them. Owner sign‑off at a gate means later changes go through the same gate again. Hover or click any node.

    ▾
    Mahogany edges and nodes are the owner’s territory: gates, ratification, and the paste that starts the campaign.

    What the gates hunt for here

    Not build defects but research defects: a search prompt whose domain the methodology does not cover, an extraction prompt that cannot fill the evidence schema, a stop condition no one can measure against the ledger. The audit assumes the pack fails when executed and looks for where.

    A and B are never one document

    Prompt A is the owner’s input — ratified scope, the null hypothesis, the frozen/seed/live inventory. Prompt B is the machine that turns it into artifacts. Keeping them separate makes the review gate between them real: B is audited against A, and sign‑off has a definite object.

    04

    Anatomy of a research pack#

    Every artifact the pipeline produces. All are governed documents with allocated codes — except exactly one.

    ▾
    The one untracked artifact. The kick‑off paragraph is delivered in chat so the owner can paste it into the next terminal without hunting for files. Its durable copy is the kick‑off record’s final section — the chat delivery is a convenience copy, and everything else on this page is governed.
    05

    Phases follow the pass structure#

    Backlog phases are sized one session each and dependency‑ordered along the content methodology’s four passes — so no synthesis can start while its evidence phases are open.

    ▾
    The pass chain is encoded in depends_on, not left to judgment; the section shape repeats inside every phase.

    OrderingEvidence before synthesis

    Broad mapping before deep reading, deep reading before adversarial testing, adversarial testing before synthesis. The dependency encoding makes the order mechanical: the queue itself refuses a synthesis phase whose evidence phases are open.

    CompletionSaturation is demonstrated

    The content methodology’s stop conditions become the completion gate, measured against the reproducibility ledger — searches returning duplicates, every hypothesis with at least one serious challenger. Saturation is never declared; it is shown.

    06

    Standing rules#

    Seven rule groups that travel with every campaign, whatever it investigates.

    ▾

    ContextSelective injection

    Context loads when the step that needs it begins, never up front. Search prompts carry their own domain and terminology variants, not the whole matrix; the coordinator defers the content methodology to the agents that execute it. Nothing front‑loads the full pack into any agent’s opening context.

    ThesisThesis discipline

    The campaign works to support the null hypothesis. Collisions are recorded first; architecture is revised only in synthesis. No agent renames a concept or narrows a hypothesis to dodge a collision — a hypothesis change mid‑campaign is a blocking finding, not an edit. The frozen baseline is never modified, and “no prior work exists” is not a permitted verdict.

    EvidenceEvidence hygiene

    Every search appends to the reproducibility ledger — query, provider, date, filters, results, rationale. A search that logged nothing did not happen. Generated summaries are leads, never evidence; untraceable claims are removed, not softened; derivative sources are tracked to their shared ancestor and never counted as independent confirmation.

    DisciplineAgent hygiene

    The coordinator claims nothing, searches nothing, writes no findings. Search, extraction and review are separate dispatches, and reviewers receive sources and evidence rows — never the searcher’s rationale. Findings commit before review; truncated agents are resumed, not re‑run; assertions are verified against the ledger.

    SpendCost protocols

    Haiku for mechanical gates. Sonnet is the standard model for search and extraction — and the default for synthesis and collision review. Opus is never pre‑assigned: at most one documented escalation per campaign. Two fix cycles per item; what survives them is reported, not looped on.

    CadenceGate check‑in policy

    Prompt A always asks the owner up front: stop at gates for check‑in, or push through to close‑out? The answer lands in the kick‑off record, never assumed. A critical issue — a collision that falsifies scope, a methodology defect that invalidates collected evidence — first gets a dual review, and pauses for the owner only if it survives unresolved.

    CompletionStop conditions and descope

    The content methodology’s stop conditions become the completion gate, measured against the ledger — saturation is demonstrated, never declared. The descope ladder exists for emergencies, but no rung is taken without the owner’s explicit direction unless the kick‑off record grants a stated exception. A campaign that stops early stops at a phase boundary, with its resume state in the session record.

    And always
    • Seed sources stay seed sources until they pass the verification steps the content methodology requires; the historical seed ledger is never silently altered.
    • Negative claims use the status vocabulary — a failed search is recorded as a search that failed, with its queries.
    • Every document code comes from the allocator (--next-code).
