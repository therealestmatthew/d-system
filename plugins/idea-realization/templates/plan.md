# Replace with the plan's title

dates. Replace every paragraph below; keep the headings, which plan-check reads.

## Context and scope

The observed problem, where the evidence for it is, and what this plan covers.

## Decisions

Each decision, the alternative it rejected, and what that alternative would have cost. Say who
decided each one and whether it is settled.

## Work and dependencies

## Acceptance and verification

on a requirement document, retitle this section `Requirement coverage` and map each requirement
row to the step that meets it.

## Out of scope

## Open questions

when nothing is open.
