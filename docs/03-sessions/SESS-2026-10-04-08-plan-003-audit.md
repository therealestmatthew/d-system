## Decisions

- **The owner retired the authored YAML content site** rather than deferring or keeping it.
  Retirement made the scope's conditional ADR mandatory, so `docs/04-decisions/` was added to the
  deliverables in the claim commit, with the owner's approval.
- **The owner ruled the stranded dependents be reported, not edited.** `phase-syn-05` and
  `phase-sch-06` went to Ideation as `000567` and `000568`. Three other follow-ups went the same
  way: `000569` error boundary, `000570` CI ruff on `tools/`, and `000571` the `sys-html` registry.
- **`deprecated`, not `superseded`, for the seven plans.** Nothing replaces the product they
  describe. A `superseded` document must also be named in a replacement's `supersedes` list.
- **The dispositions live at the end of each plan document**, next to the requirements they
  dispose of, rather than in a separate audit file. Putting them at the end keeps the line numbers
  they cite valid.
- **Ruled-out requirements are marked retired.** The owner's ruling turned every "open, if the
  product is kept" into retired. Requirements GOV-003 had already overruled (permissive contracts)
  are retired too, since nothing was built in their place.
- **Requirements met by unrelated work are marked accomplished.** PyYAML, the `/api` proxy,
  `StrictMode` and the CI gates are credited to the commits or CI lines that provide them, with a
  note where no `phase-html-*` phase was involved.
